The author

Meet JP Bohler.

JP Bohler writes about improving basketball shooting through practical home training. Perfect Your Basketball Shot At Home brings together a personal shooting journey, baseline measurement, home practice techniques, mental focus and progress tracking for recreational players.

The goal is purposeful practice that fits ordinary life. Results depend on the player, practice and access to suitable equipment.
